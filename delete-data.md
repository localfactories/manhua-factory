# Deleting your data

Manhua Factory, the Facebook app Shadow Manhua Uploader, keeps no data on any server: there is no server. The only things it stores are the sign-in tokens and a list of the videos it has posted, on the owner's own computer.

To remove the app's access on Facebook and Instagram, open Facebook, go to Settings and privacy, Settings, Apps and websites, and remove Shadow Manhua Uploader. For Google, remove it at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

To have any data deleted, email shadowmanhua@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data. We delete the token files and the posting record and confirm by email within 30 days.
