# Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

## Who we are

Manhua Factory is a private tool run by Local Factories to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channels (Shadow Manhua and Faris Fun Storytime). It is not offered to the public. Contact: shadowmanhua@gmail.com

## What the tool uses from your Google account

When the channel owner signs in, the tool asks YouTube for permission to manage that channel's videos. It uses the YouTube Data API only to read the channel's name (to confirm it is the right channel), to upload our own videos and set their title, description, thumbnail, subtitles, playlist and publishing time, and to read the channel's own playlists and scheduled videos so that nothing is uploaded twice.

## Where the data is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool keeps a local record of which videos it has uploaded. Nothing is sent to any server of ours: there is no server, no analytics and no advertising.

## Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data from Google or YouTube to anyone. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes it from the tool.
