# Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

## Who we are

Manhua Factory is a private tool run by Local Factories to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channels, Shadow Manhua and Faris Fun Storytime, and to our own Facebook Pages and their linked Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public. Contact: shadowmanhua@gmail.com

## What the tool uses from your Google account

When the channel owner signs in, the tool asks YouTube for permission to manage that channel's videos. It uses the YouTube Data API only to read the channel's name to confirm it is the right channel, to upload our own videos and set their title, description, thumbnail, subtitles, playlist and publishing time, and to read the channel's own playlists and scheduled videos so that nothing is uploaded twice.

## What the tool uses from Facebook and Instagram

When the Page owner signs in with Facebook, the app Shadow Manhua Uploader asks Meta for permission to publish to the owner's own Facebook Page and its linked Instagram professional account. It uses the Facebook Graph API only to read the names of the owner's Pages to pick the right Page, to post our own videos and Reels to that Page and Instagram account, to set their title, description, cover and publishing time, and to read the Page's own scheduled videos so that nothing is posted twice. It does not read messages, friends, followers or any other person's data.

## Where the data is kept

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool keeps a local record of which videos it has posted. Nothing is sent to any server of ours: there is no server, no analytics and no advertising.

## Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data from Google, YouTube, Facebook or Instagram to anyone. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy at https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy including the Limited Use requirements.

## YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Removing access and deleting your data

Access can be removed at any time: for Google at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and for Facebook and Instagram in Facebook Settings, Apps and websites. Deleting the token files on the owner's computer also removes them from the tool. How to have any data deleted is explained at https://localfactories.github.io/manhua-factory/delete-data.html or email shadowmanhua@gmail.com
